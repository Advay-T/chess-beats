    How it works

    Each move

    File a–h Note in the pentatonic scale
    Rank 1–8 Octave, low to high
    Side White: bright synth, left. Black: darker FM synth, right.
    Piece A pawn plays short and soft, a queen long and loud

    Special moves

    Capture Accent and snare
    Check Cymbal
    Castling Three-note chord
    Promotion Rising run
    Checkmate Low chord, kick and cymbal

    Layers

    Motion Knights leap. Rooks, bishops and queens glide along moves of 3+ squares.
    Harmony The pad chord tracks material: open when level, major when White leads, minor when Black leads, tense after a swing of 3+.
    Groove Silence, then hi-hats, then kick and bass, then a full beat as captures and checks pile up.
    Phase The opening stays sparse. The middlegame gets everything. The endgame slows down and echoes.

    Auto key

    Tactical games play in a minor key, positional games in major. The opening moves pick the key, so two games with the same opening share it. Every game starts at 200 BPM. Choose any key, scale or tempo to override.

    Timeline

    Background colour shows the phase, orange shows tension, and the white line shows material. Red ticks mark captures, yellow ticks mark checks. Click anywhere on it to jump.
