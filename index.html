    How moves become music
    • File (a–h) → note in the pentatonic scale
    • Rank (1–8) → octave (low to high)
    • White = bright synth, left · Black = dark FM synth, right
    • Piece: pawn short & soft → queen long & loud
    • Capture = accent + snare · Check = cymbal
    • Castling = chord · Promotion = rising run
    • Checkmate = final big hit
