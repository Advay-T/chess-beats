Chess as music · 1,003 famous games

Every chess game has a melody.

Each move plays a note, every capture hits a snare, and checkmate lands like a final chord. Chess Beats plays famous games from Morphy to Gukesh as music in your browser.

01 · The mixing desk

Every move is a note.

The square a piece lands on sets the pitch. The piece sets the length. Captures, checks and mates each have their own channel. Watch the strips light up as the Immortal Game plays above.

02 · The score sheet

The whole game shapes the song.

Before the first note, Chess Beats reads the entire game: material, tension and phase after every move. Four layers turn that into an arrangement.

  1. MotionKnights leap, long moves glide up to their square.
  2. HarmonyA pad whose chord follows who is ahead.
  3. GrooveA beat that builds as the tension rises.
  4. PhaseSparse in the opening, slower with echo in the endgame.
